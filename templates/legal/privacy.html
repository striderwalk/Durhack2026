3. Why we use it

Each purpose below has a proposed lawful basis under UK data protection law, for review. [TO CONFIRM ALL BASES]

  • Running your account and the matching features (your profile, interests, connections): to provide the service you asked for. Proposed basis: contract. [TO CONFIRM]
  • Checking you're a Durham student (the @durham.ac.uk address): to keep the service for students. Proposed basis: legitimate interests. [TO CONFIRM]
  • Safety and moderation (reports, blocks, bans, the audit log, the email fingerprint for bans): to protect users. Proposed basis: legitimate interests. [TO CONFIRM]
  • Security (sign-in attempt limits, session cookie): to protect accounts. Proposed basis: legitimate interests. [TO CONFIRM]
  • Recording that you accepted the terms: to show you agreed. Proposed basis: contract / legitimate interests. [TO CONFIRM]

6. How long we keep it

  • Most of your data is kept until your account is deleted. The app does not yet delete accounts itself (see Your rights).
  • If an account is banned, the ban record is kept even after the account is deleted, with a one-way fingerprint of the email address, so the ban still applies if the person registers again. [RETENTION PERIOD TO CONFIRM]
  • Reports, the moderator audit log and terms acceptances: [RETENTION PERIODS TO CONFIRM]
  • Sign-in attempt records: 15 minutes, in memory only.

7. Your rights

You can ask to:

  • see the data we hold about you;
  • correct it (you can change most of it yourself in your profile and Settings);
  • delete it;
  • object to how we use it.

To make a request, contact {{ LEGAL.contact }}. The app does not yet have a "delete my account" button, so deletion is done on request. [REQUEST AND DELETION PROCESS TO CONFIRM]

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Security

What we do now:

  • passwords are stored only in a one-way scrambled form;
  • every page needs you to be signed in, and the server (not just the page) checks who can see what;
  • contact details are only ever sent to your connections;
  • repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down;
  • moderators' views of email addresses are logged.

No system is completely secure. If you think your account or data has been misused, contact {{ LEGAL.contact }} straight away. [HTTPS AND HOSTING SECURITY TO CONFIRM]
