1. What this service is

Durham Superposition helps Durham students find other students who share their interests. You can add interests to your profile, see who else has them, create new interests, and share contact details with people you connect with.

It is run by {{ LEGAL.operator }} as a student project.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be a current student at Durham University.
  • You must register with your student @durham.ac.uk email address. Staff cannot use the service.
  • You must be 18 or over. [MINIMUM AGE TO CONFIRM]
  • One account per person. Don't create accounts for anyone else.

[TO CONFIRM] At the moment the service checks that an address ends in @durham.ac.uk, but it does not yet send a code to prove you own the address, and it cannot yet tell staff from students.

4. Your account

  • You sign in with your email address or username and a password. [TO CONFIRM: update this if sign-in moves to one-time codes sent to your email]
  • Keep your password private. Don't share your account or let anyone else use it.
  • If you think someone else has used your account, tell us at {{ LEGAL.contact }}.

5. Using the service: the rules

Don't use the service to:

  • harass, bully, threaten or intimidate anyone;
  • post hateful content, or content that attacks people for who they are;
  • post sexual content;
  • pretend to be someone else;
  • send spam or advertising;
  • post anything illegal;
  • copy or collect other people's information, including by automated scraping;
  • share someone else's contact details without their permission;
  • misuse connection requests ("waves"), for example to pester someone who hasn't responded.

Interests you create must be genuine interests that other students could share.

6. What you post

You own what you post: your profile, bio, interests and contact details. You let us show it to other users in the ways the service describes (for example, your profile to signed-in students, and your contact details only to your connections). This permission ends when your content is removed, except where we keep records as described in the Information on Data and Security.

You are responsible for what you post. Make sure you have the right to post it.

7. Safety, reporting and moderation

  • Report a person or an interest using the Report options. A moderator will review it. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Block someone from their profile. Neither of you will then see the other's profile or appear on each other's interest pages, and any connection between you ends. They are not told.
  • Moderators can remove interests and can suspend or ban accounts that break these rules.
  • We don't check everything people post, and we're not responsible for what users post or for what happens when users meet or share contact details.

Stay safe. If you meet someone from the service, meet in a public place, tell a friend where you're going, and leave if you feel uncomfortable. This is advice, not a guarantee of safety.

8. Bans

  • A moderator can ban an account for 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or permanently.
  • While banned you are signed out, can't sign in, and see a screen explaining the ban and, for a temporary ban, when it ends.
  • A record of the ban is kept, including a one-way fingerprint of your email address, so that a ban still applies if you register again. See the Information on Data and Security.
  • To appeal a ban, contact {{ LEGAL.contact }}. [APPEALS PROCESS TO CONFIRM]
